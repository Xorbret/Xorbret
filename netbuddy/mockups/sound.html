Sound Set // MitamaOS

Aspect 3. Tap any card to hear it. Now the cues glide and sing — portamento (continuous pitch slides), vibrato and legato swells give the Mitama inflection instead of staccato beeps, GLaDOS-style. On device these render as short synthesized PCM (the ESP32 drives the waveform directly), not plain tone() beeps. Each maps to a §14 mood/event.

headphones/volume up — piezo tones are quiet & short
Design intent: curt, synthetic, a little smug. Up = good, down = disappointment, alternating = alarm. The alarm is the only harsh one (Fiend/full-screen takeover); everything else stays light so the OS isn't noisy. All of it obeys the UI-sound on/off and volume settings, and nothing audio ever gates the UI.

Tunable: pitch, length, and number of notes per cue. Tell me which to make brighter, shorter, meaner, or to cut.
