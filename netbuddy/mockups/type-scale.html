Bake plan: Rajdhani converted to M5GFX bitmap fonts at 3 core sizes — 8 / 10 / 14px — plus one 20px boot size. Body(9) renders from the 10px face down-hinted, or we add a 4th bake if 9 looks muddy.

Honest caveat: baked fonts are 1-bit (no anti-aliasing), so 8px is the floor — it reads here in a browser with AA, but on-device the Small/Badge sizes may need hand-tuned glyphs or bumping Small to 9px. We confirm this on real hardware at M0.

Tunable: nudge any role ±1px, change a weight, or drop the Badge tier into Small. Tell me what to change.
