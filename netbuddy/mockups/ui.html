MitamaOS // UI Mockups

Every screen is rendered at the real 240×135 of the Cardputer, scaled up. Rajdhani, Night City surface over a red-bones grid, 16px top bar + 14px status bar (PaperOS's real dimensions). Layout proposals for discussion — not final art. Fixed: the status-bar line is now one line that marquee-scrolls on device when longer than the bar (shown here truncated with …), and the top bar dropped the overflowing battery %.

01 Boot / POST
COMP power-on. Magatama + wordmark on the red grid, a segmented loader, and a GLaDOS-flavored boot line.
02 Launcher
App grid with the live top bar (clock / Wi-Fi / battery). Selected tile glows yellow with a red corner-tick. Mitama rides the status bar with a one-liner.
03 Environment Status
The Mitama's home screen: big sprite + aura, live network summary, recent sightings, and its current mood line. Calm/green here = all clear.
04 Compendium
Demon registry: race-grouped list on the left, selected entry card on the right (race, rarity, confidence, count, quip). SMT card framing in purple/magenta.
05 Fiend Alert
Hostile event. Red bones surge through, glitch bars, one clipped line — then the facts, never buried. This is the "snark yields to clarity" rule on screen.
06 Mitama toast (Clippy intrusion)
Unsolicited help over any app (Notes shown behind). A corner Mitama + speech strip. Dismiss with ESC; it will judge you either way.
Open questions these raise: launcher as grid (shown) vs scrolling list · whether the Mitama is always-docked on the status bar or only on its own screen · Compendium split-view (shown) vs list→full-card drill-in. Say which way you lean and I'll tighten the chosen ones.
