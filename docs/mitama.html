Mitama // Design Study

A faceless companion spirit. No eyes, no mouth — ever. It emotes through tilt, aura, a single sigil, and motion (the GLaDOS trick). Night City palette painted over a red "bones" frame. Concepts only — calibration, not final art.

Base forms

Four silhouettes for the core spirit. Each carries one sigil (a magatama/comma mark) — a crest, never a face — and an aura ring whose brightness is the "optic." Pick one to build the expression system and outfits on.

Magatama sigil — pick the icon

The single mark the Mitama wears. The eye is gone (it read as an ouroboros) — these are solid open commas, the true magatama/soul shape. Whichever you pick becomes the Mitama's crest everywhere (and the app icon).

Expression states — same mask, no face

Proof of the no-face doctrine: this is the recommended base, unchanged, emoting only through tilt, aura colour/intensity, sigil pulse, vertical lean and glitch. Nothing on a "face" moves because there isn't one.

Outfits / skins

Costumes layer onto the same base. They change silhouette add-ons, accent colour and aura flavour — the expression system underneath is identical, so personality never changes with the outfit.

What an outfit may & may not touch

An outfit MAY change

  • Silhouette add-ons (horns, ears, hat, cord, visor bar)
  • Accent colour pulled from the palette
  • Aura shape & idle-motion flavour
  • A cosmetic frame motif around the card

An outfit must NEVER

  • Add eyes, a mouth, or any face
  • Replace the sigil + tilt + aura expression system
  • Break legibility at ~40px (top-bar size)
  • Remove the red-bones frame or add real menace

Palette — surface over bones

Expression lives in motion + the written line-bank, not pixels on a face — so every costume above tells the exact same jokes. Swap any shape, colour or name; the method holds.
