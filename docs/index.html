MitamaOS

A companion that watches your network

A defensive, receive-only handheld OS for the M5 Cardputer ADV. A gentle spirit — the Mitama — rides along, reading the radio world around you and flagging what's watching back. It detects and informs; it never attacks.

Meet the Mitama

A faceless companion spirit — no eyes, no mouth, ever. It emotes through tilt, aura, a single magatama sigil and motion (the GLaDOS trick). Its voice is Clippy × GLaDOS: snarky, dry, and quietly, competently right. It drifts with the clock and your session — sleepy after 9pm, weary after four hours — and only the alarm is ever harsh.

Decided so far

  • Name MitamaOS
  • License GPLv2 · open source
  • Base PaperOS core · SquachWatch detection · AdvanceOS themes
  • Hardware Cardputer ADV · ESP32-S3 · 8MB · no PSRAM
  • Home Proxima (companion layer)
  • Dex the Compendium · demon races · Fiends
  • Mascot faceless Soul · magatama crest
  • Voice Clippy × GLaDOS · authored lines + LLM flavor
  • Mood time + fatigue temperament · sass dial
  • A11y Calm Mode · flash-safe · facts never hidden

Design is being locked aspect by aspect before any firmware is written. Full running record in the design doc.
